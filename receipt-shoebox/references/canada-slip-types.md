# Canadian slip and receipt types

Common names the user can pass as `needed_types`. Source: Canada Revenue Agency, "Preparing to do your taxes" (canada.ca). Most people receive only a few of these.

| Type | What it shows |
|---|---|
| T4 | Employment income and payroll deductions |
| T4A | Pension, scholarship, RESP payments, and other income |
| T4E | Employment Insurance and other benefits |
| T5 | Investment income such as bank interest |
| T3 | Trust income, such as some fund distributions |
| T5007 | Workers' compensation, social assistance, or provincial supplements |
| T5008 | Securities transactions |
| T2202 | Tuition and enrolment certificate |
| RRSP contribution receipt | RRSP contributions |
| Child care receipt | Child care expenses |
| Medical receipt | Eligible medical expenses |
| Donation receipt | Charitable donations |

Issuers generally send slips before March 1 of the following year. The CRA asks people to keep receipts and records for at least six years after filing. The Skill only sorts and lists files; it does not give tax advice.
