Receipt Shoebox

A UGREEN AI NAS Skill that turns one messy folder of receipts, bills and tax slips into a tidy year-end folder, and tells you which slips are still missing. This page is a dry run on made-up sample files, so you can see each step and the approval gate without a NAS.

How the Skill works on a NAS

file-agent finds candidates with ug-filesystem ls and find. document-agent runs ug-document extract with the receipt, invoice or expense schema and dedupe on. photo-agent runs ug-photo vision-ocr on images with no text, and document-agent reads that OCR text. Nothing is copied until you approve. Then file-agent copies with ug-filesystem mkdir and cpmv, and knowledge-agent saves the summary with ug-knowledge save-text. Source files are never moved or deleted.

Read the Skill: SKILL.md. Slip names come from the Canada Revenue Agency page Preparing to do your taxes. This tool sorts files; it does not give tax advice.
